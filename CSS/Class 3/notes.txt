Topic 2: Type of Positions
5 Type of positions:

i. Static : The default position of any object.In this position we cannot move any object.

ii. Absolute : In this it moves from his parent space and leave his space.

iii. Relative : In this it moves from its own space and do not leave his space.

iv. Fixed : In this it leaves his space and fix at one place.

v. Sticky : It will get stick at one place but do not leave his space.
